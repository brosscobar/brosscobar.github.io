Building design
for multi-brand growth.

From siloed design work to a team of 10, a reusable platform and faster casino launches.

Design Operations & Team Lead
Mandate

10+ casino brands, mobile and desktop
Period

The starting point

One casino was becoming
a portfolio of brands.

I joined GoWild during its transition from a single casino to a multi-brand operation. Design was fragmented: individual designers worked in silos, each responsible for a casino, with no shared approach.

My mandate was to build a design organization that could support rapid growth. That meant changing how the team worked, creating a reusable product foundation and improving the player journeys that mattered to the business.

The results

A stronger team.
A faster path to launch.

Design operations

Build the organization
behind the output.

I built and led a team of 10 designers, establishing hiring pipelines, onboarding, career frameworks and quarterly and annual performance reviews. I recognized exceptional work through bonuses and gave designers a clearer path to develop their craft and careers.

Standardized workflows, intake templates and request processes gave Marketing, Development and Product a shared way to work with Design. Clearer priorities and production expectations helped the team manage concurrent campaigns and platform launches.

White-label architecture

Design the foundation once.
Make room for every brand.

The defining piece of work was a modular white-label design system. Reusable UI frameworks could be adapted through colors, typography, logos and thematic assets, giving new casinos a starting point for design and implementation.

I developed brand books, templates and visual guidelines to keep each casino’s identity distinct while maintaining technical consistency and design quality. New-brand launch cycles fell from 6–9 months to around 3 months.

Research & optimization

Make player behavior
part of the design process.

I embedded Hotjar analytics and A/B testing into the team’s workflow across regions and devices. Funnel mapping and behavioral evidence helped us identify friction, test changes and improve key journeys.

The work focused on registration, account access, deposits and mobile navigation, connecting interface decisions to conversion and engagement.

Registration & account access

Remove friction
before the first game.

Systematic journey mapping and UX optimization reduced registration abandonment by 40%. The registration work simplified the first screen and brought welcome-offer selection into the third step.

I also mapped login, activation and account-recovery paths, documenting the account states and exceptions engineers needed to implement the experience.

Payments & mobile

Make important actions
easier to complete.

I designed and launched a one-click fast-deposit experience that increased deposit conversions. For the broader cashier journey, I worked with Finance and Engineering on payment layouts, fewer steps and clear success, error and limit states.

I led a mobile-first overhaul of site architecture and navigation. The responsive game grid and promotion hierarchy helped the platform adapt across devices while keeping discovery and key actions visible.

Cross-functional delivery

Turn parallel work
into a shared process.

I partnered with Engineering, Marketing and business stakeholders to align design output with launch timelines. Standardized asset handoffs and production templates helped the team support campaigns and new platforms at the same time.

The same collaboration extended to internal tools. I gathered requirements from CRM users and VIP managers and worked with project managers and engineers on clearer operational interfaces.

Brand expression & motion

Carry each identity
through the experience.

The shared framework supported different visual personalities across the casino portfolio. Brand guidelines and production templates connected product interfaces, campaigns and marketing assets.

I also created these Wild Jackpot and Piggy Bang animations in After Effects, extending their visual identities through character, movement and pacing.

What changed

Design became
a capability for growth.

GoWild gained a unified design organization, a reusable foundation for 10+ brands and a repeatable delivery process. The work connected team development and brand architecture with hands-on improvements to registration, deposits and mobile navigation.

My role combined design operations, creative direction and product UX, building both the team and the systems needed to keep delivering as the portfolio grew.
